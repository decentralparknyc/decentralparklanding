Novel Digital Affordances in Labor Rights and Organizing

What if we made work more humane instead of less — protecting workers and evening the playing field? An evening imagining worker-first technology: Ostrom-aligned monitoring, verifiable anonymous whistleblowing, handling of mental load and invisible labor, augmented conflict resolution, and real sovereignty over our own labor and outputs. Curiosity and skepticism both welcome.
